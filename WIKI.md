## Introduction
WinCurl 3.0 introduces a massive overhaul, featuring a 3D isometric view, advanced physics, dynamic UI, network matchmaking, and an epic story mode! 

## Game Modes
- **Quick Play (Local)**: Play a classic curling match against another player locally.
- **Quick Play (Bot)**: Challenge the WinCurl AI. Features three difficulty tiers.
- **Multiplayer (Host/Join)**: Play against a friend over the network. Uses robust netcode for seamless stone synchronization.
- **Story Mode**: A sprawling single-player campaign where you must defeat the tech-bro villains of the Curling Club circuit.
- **Challenge Mode**: Sharpen your skills with various target-based minigames and sweeping challenges.

## Gameplay Mechanics
- **Flick-to-Throw**: Throw stones intuitively using a natural forward drag or "flick" motion on the screen. The harder and further you flick forward, the more momentum the stone gains.
- **WYSIWYG Trajectory**: The trajectory preview line simulates the exact path of the stone up to maximum power and curl, dynamically caching for maximum performance.

## Save & Resume System
WinCurl 3.0 now supports a fully-featured **Save & Resume System** for both 1v1 Bot matches and Story Mode.
- You can save your game directly from the Pause Menu in supported modes.
- Saves are managed in 3 distinct slots, presented in a retro 16-bit interface.
- Exiting a match without saving will **not** wipe your previous save data! The data is only overridden when you explicitly save over a slot.

## Settings & Optimization
- **Hi-Res Mode (Android Only)**: For high-end devices, enabling this will use your device's native resolution and enable GPU bilinear filtering for crisp graphics! It's off by default to save battery and boost performance on older devices.
- **Optimization**: The game uses intelligent UI caching, trajectory caching, and event filtering to maintain a buttery smooth 120 FPS across modern displays!

## Story Mode
The curling world is under threat! WinCurl's Story Mode places you in a world where modern curling clubs and the very concept of ice and the environment are under threat by profit-hungry tech tycoons, hyper-capitalist investors, and the military-industrial complex. 

It acts as an escalating gauntlet, where each opponent has a different AI playstyle (aggressive, defensive, chaotic, or balanced) and varying difficulty. Your goal is to defeat them in Curling to save the environment. 

### Characters & Opponents
Here is a breakdown of the characters and their associated arenas:

1. **CEO Smogsworth (Corporate Lobby)**
   * **Difficulty:** 3
   * **AI Type:** Aggressive
   * **Overview:** A classic hyper-capitalist billionaire. His factories produce 90% of the world's carbon emissions. He just bought your curling club and the concept of ice itself with the intent of paving over the rink to build a toxic waste incinerator.

2. **Tremor (Crypto Mine Rink)**
   * **Difficulty:** 5
   * **AI Type:** Defensive
   * **Overview:** A crypto-bro mining magnate who is literally melting the polar ice caps to liquid-cool his blockchain rigs. He hates the traditional curling club because it's standing in the way of unregulated hyper-capitalism and his Bitcoin farm.

3. **Poly Mer (Social Media Hub)**
   * **Difficulty:** 6
   * **AI Type:** Chaotic
   * **Overview:** A chaotic, hyper-online social media influencer. She intends to tear down the ozone layer to build a giant 5G hype house and threatens to sell your curling strategies to the highest bidder to maximize her digital engagement. 

4. **EliteFour (The War Room)**
   * **Difficulty:** 7
   * **AI Type:** Balanced
   * **Overview:** Represents the military-industrial complex. They view curling merely as a simulation for drone strikes and orbital lasers. To them, endless war is profit, and they threaten to wipe your civilization off the map with tactical strikes if you cross the hog line.

5. **Dr. Sludge (AI Startup Arena)**
   * **Difficulty:** 7
   * **AI Type:** Aggressive
   * **Overview:** An arrogant AI tech-founder who recently raised $50 billion to replace you with a curling robot. His predictive LLMs have decided that humanity is inefficient, and you're just training data for his algorithms.

6. **Timber Baroness (Metaverse Dome)**
   * **Difficulty:** 8
   * **AI Type:** Defensive
   * **Overview:** A dystopian VR tycoon who has enslaved millions in virtual reality factories to mine digital resources. While the real world burns, she prefers selling virtual water drops and encourages everyone to accept the "new digital reality".

7. **Baron Von Crude (Big Data Complex)**
   * **Difficulty:** 9
   * **AI Type:** Chaotic
   * **Overview:** A massive data-broker mastermind who controls the water, the air, and global data. He has engineered famines just to watch the stock market fluctuate and has indexed every possible curling move you could make.

8. **Syntax Terror (Cloud Host Club)**
   * **Difficulty:** 10
   * **AI Type:** Balanced
   * **Overview:** The ultimate boss. He is the architect of the global data grid. He controls the cloud, the data, and the physics of the curling rink itself. He believes your archaic physical stones stand no chance against his optimized algorithms.

## Multiplayer Chat & Text Formatting
WinCurl features an interactive live chat during multiplayer matches! To access chat, tap the **Chat** icon or press `T` or `Enter` on your keyboard.

**Quake-Style Color Formatting**
You can spice up your chat messages and your player username using Quake-style color codes! Just type `^` followed by a number from `0-9` before your text to change its color:

* `^0` - Black
* `^1` - Red
* `^2` - Green
* `^3` - Yellow
* `^4` - Blue
* `^5` - Cyan
* `^6` - Magenta
* `^7` - White
* `^8` - Gray
* `^9` - Dark Gray

For example, typing `^1Hello ^4World` will make "Hello" red and "World" blue! This works both in in-game chat and when choosing your display name in the multiplayer lobby!
